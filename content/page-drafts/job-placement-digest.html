Investigation · WordPress AI

Finding what the request log missed

Role Reported and tested by me · fix by Anubhav Anand

Status ○ Fix in review

Codex generations never appeared in the WordPress AI request log, and nothing errored, so the log looked complete when it was not. I traced the gap to logging that wrapped one SDK HTTP transporter: providers that sent requests through a sidecar, a custom handler or a direct WordPress HTTP call never reached it.

I wrote the report and reproduction, then integration-tested the fix as it stood then. That version restored the missing successes, but it double-logged them while the provider bridge stayed active, and it still dropped failures.

How I work

I reproduce the problem, trace it to the code, test the change and leave a record the next person can act on. Each example above links to the work behind it and says whether I built it, reported it or tested it.

I choose roles by the same standard. The work should survive inspection by someone who is not me, the customer’s problem should be operational WordPress at scale, and the role should be a rung toward customer-facing technical leadership, not a detour.

My customer-facing record is WordPress.com support in 2012 and one named client delivery, DJ Lee & Voices of Judah. I do not yet have a public enterprise-scale monitoring or incident record.
